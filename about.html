Our story

Independent advice, personal support.

Muthige Insurance Brokers was founded in 2024 by Orifha Muthige, an FSCA-licensed independent insurance intermediary based in Rosebank, Johannesburg.

The brokerage was started to give individuals and small business owners in Gauteng an alternative to call-centre insurers — someone who compares policies across multiple insurers and stays personally involved through the claims process.

The practice currently operates with Orifha and one junior broker, with the goal of building a client base of around 50 policyholders in its first year.
